Statutory Governance under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009

The Legal Metrology Division operates under the Department of Consumer Affairs, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution. Central legislation governs standard units, model approvals, and national policy, while State and UT Directorates carry out periodic verification, market surveillance, and legal enforcement.
