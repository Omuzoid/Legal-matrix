National Legal Metrology Digital Services • Statutory Framework: Legal Metrology Act, 2009

1. Use of Service

Legal Matrix provides electronic facilitation for legal metrology verification, digital certificate generation, application tracking, and consumer grievance submission. Access to this platform is granted subject to strict compliance with these terms and applicable provisions of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, and the Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011.

2. User Responsibilities

Users of this platform undertake that:

  • All information provided regarding merchant identity, commercial premises, and instrument technical specifications is accurate, complete, and legally truthful.
  • Measuring instruments submitted for verification are maintained in clean condition and presented at the scheduled time with necessary manufacturer documentation.
  • No attempt will be made to alter or tamper with electronic seals, serial numbers, or certification outputs.

3. Service Availability

While the system is architected for continuous public availability, the administration does not warrant uninterrupted service during scheduled maintenance windows, infrastructure updates, or unexpected network outages. Offline draft facilities are provided to protect user entries during transient connectivity issues.

4. Application Processing and Stamping

Online registration of an instrument does not constitute certification. Certification is concluded only after an authorized Legal Metrology Officer has performed physical verification using standard test weights, computed Maximum Permissible Error tolerance conformance, and affixed the requisite seal.

5. Digital Certificates and QR Verification

Certificates generated through Legal Matrix bear a unique verification identifier and digital QR token. Consumers and statutory enforcement personnel may scan this token to inspect live certificate status. A digital certificate becomes invalid immediately upon expiration of the statutory validity period or upon formal revocation by an enforcement officer.

6. Prohibited Activities

Users shall not:

  • Submit fraudulent or fabricated measuring instrument serial numbers.
  • Impersonate an authorized Legal Metrology inspector, calibration officer, or administrative controller.
  • Introduce malicious software, automated extraction scripts, or automated load testing tools without authorization.
  • Mislead consumers using expired, copied, or forged verification certificates.
